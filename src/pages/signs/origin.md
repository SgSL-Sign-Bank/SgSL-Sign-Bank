## Variation 1

Same motion as the [first variation of "begin"](./begin#variation-1), which is
shown below, but use the "O" hand sign instead of the "B" hand sign.

![Variation 1 of begin](@signs/begin-variation-1.gif)

## Variation 2

Same motion as the [first variation](#variation-1), but place the index finger
on the skin between the thumb and the index finger, or the web of your thumb on
your other hand.
