## Variation 2

Same motion as the [first variation](#variation-1), but loop the moving hand
once more after the motion ends. This variation is more common than the
[first](#variation-1).

![Variation 2 of college](@signs/college-variation-2.gif)

## Variation 3

Same motion as the [first variation](#variation-1), but use the "C" hand sign
instead of the open palm.

![Variation 3 of college](@signs/college-variation-3.gif)

## Variation 4

Same motion as the [third variation](#variation-3), which is shown above, but
loop the moving hand once more after the motion ends.
