## Variation 1

Same motion as the [first variation of "college"](./college#variation-1), but
use the "U" hand sign instead of the open palm.

![Variation 1 of university](@signs/university-variation-1.gif)

## Variation 2

Same motion as the [first variation](#variation-1), which is shown above, but
loop the moving hand once more after the motion ends. This variation is more
common than the [first](#variation-1).
