## Variation 1

Use this variation to mean that something is full, like a bag or a cup.

![Variation 1 of full](@signs/full-variation-1.gif)

The hand moves towards your body, not away from your body. Moving the hand away
means ["enough"](./enough.md) instead.

## Variation 2

Use this variation to mean that you are full after eating or drinking.

![Variation 2 of full](@signs/full-variation-2.gif)
